This repository contains a collection of business-focused prompts designed to help students, researchers, and professionals use AI tools effectively for business analysis, decision-making, research, and prompt engineering.

The collection includes **100 prompts** divided into four Word documents, with **25 prompts in each file**.

## How to Use the Prompts

1. Open the required Word document.
2. Select a prompt related to your task.
3. Copy the prompt into an AI tool such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, or Perplexity.
4. Replace placeholders with your own business, company, industry, or research topic.
5. Review the AI-generated response carefully.
6. Verify important facts, statistics, sources, and citations before using the output.
7. Refine the prompt if the response does not meet your requirements.

## Recommended Prompt Customization

For better results, include the following details in a prompt whenever relevant:

- **Role:** What type of expert should the AI act as?
- **Context:** What background information does the AI need?
- **Task:** What exactly should the AI do?
- **Audience:** Who will use or read the output?
- **Constraints:** What limitations should be followed?
- **Format:** Should the answer be a table, report, presentation outline, list, or another format?
- **Evidence:** Should the AI provide sources, assumptions, or verification steps?

## Important Note on AI-Generated Research

AI-generated content may contain inaccurate information, outdated facts, or fabricated citations. Always:

- Verify sources independently.
- Check whether cited sources actually exist.
- Confirm statistics and dates.
- Prefer credible and authoritative sources.
- Distinguish facts from interpretations and recommendations.
- Do not treat AI output as automatically verified research.
